This Cookie Policy explains how Lychee Tech (“Lychee,” “we,” “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies on lycheeapp.org.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. They help sites remember preferences, keep sessions secure, and understand usage.

Cookies we use

  • Essential — language preference (lychee_lang), cookie consent choice, and security/session needs for the site to function.
  • Optional analytics — only if you accept all cookies via the banner; used to understand aggregate traffic and improve the site.

Your choices

You can accept all cookies or keep essential-only via our banner. You can also clear cookies in your browser settings at any time.

More privacy information

See our Privacy Policy for how we handle personal data more broadly.
