We're a product and engineering team shipping payments, vouchers, WhatsApp Pay, and local services across the West Bank.

How we work

  • Customer-obsessed and bilingual by default (Arabic & English).
  • Ship carefully — payments demand correctness over cleverness.
  • Respect for local context, partners, and regulators.

Open roles

We don't always list every opening publicly. If you're a strong engineer, designer, or go-to-market builder who cares about fintech in Palestine, we'd like to hear from you.

Send your CV and a short note to careers@lycheeapp.org, or use our contact form.
